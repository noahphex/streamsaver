About Streamsaver

What it does

The Coming up timeline puts your bills beside show returns for the next 60 days. Each bill shows what comes back before you pay again, helping you spot a good time to pause.

Streamsaver compares your watched shows, watchlist, streaming services, and subscription costs in one place. The goal is simple: show which services are useful right now, which ones can wait, and which ones may no longer be worth paying for.

It does not try to replace your watchlist app. It uses your viewing history as evidence so you can make cleaner decisions about subscriptions.

How it works

  1. It syncs your watched shows and watchlist from Trakt.
  2. It looks up where those shows are available using provider data.
  3. It compares that against the services you pay for or track.
  4. It scores shows based on recent activity, upcoming episodes, and whether you marked them as active, dormant, ignored, or abandoned.
  5. It turns those scores into plain recommendations about when to Pause, Resubscribe, Review renewal, or Keep.

The methodology

Streamsaver treats a streaming subscription as useful when it connects to shows you are actively watching, planning to watch, or waiting on for new episodes. A service becomes less important when its shows are old, dormant, ignored, unavailable, or duplicated by another service you already have.

The score is not meant to be perfect math. It is a practical signal. Higher scores mean a show is more likely to matter soon. Lower scores mean it is less likely to justify keeping a paid service by itself.

You stay in control. Streamsaver gives you the evidence, but it does not cancel anything for you.
