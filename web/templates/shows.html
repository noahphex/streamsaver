What do the states mean?
  • Active: watched within the last 7 days, unless a show-status rule below applies.
  • Occasional: watched within the last 30 days, but not within the last 7 days, unless a show-status rule below applies.
  • Dormant: no viewing within the last 30 days, or not seen in the sync window.
  • Anticipated: a returning series with an announced next episode or season.
  • Completed: ended or canceled, or a returning series with no next episode and no viewing for at least 30 days.
  • Abandoned: manually marked to stop counting toward recommendations; Unmark restores its previous state.
  • Ignored: excluded from service recommendations.
  • Archived: low activity, no announced return, and last watched over two years ago (or never), with ended, canceled, or unknown status.
